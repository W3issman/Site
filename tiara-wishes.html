How it works

The chama your grandmother ran, now with a receipt.

Ten people, a shared goal, and a schedule everyone can see. We just moved the ledger, the queue, and the payments onto a platform that never loses the notebook.

01

Pick your product and your plan

Browse household goods and choose cash, daily, weekly, or monthly contributions — whatever fits your income rhythm.

02

Join a chama

You're placed in a group of contributors working toward the same item. The engine manages slots, rotation order, and who's next in the queue.

03

Contribute on schedule

Pay in small amounts on your chosen cadence. Every payment is tracked and visible — no spreadsheets, no disputes.

04

Receive and repeat

When your turn comes, your item is allocated and delivered. Download your receipt, then start your next chama.
